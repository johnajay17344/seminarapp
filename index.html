Part 1 · Learn

What is software development?

SoftwareA set of instructions that tells a computer what to do.
DeveloperThe person who writes those instructions.
Think of a recipeThe developer writes it. Your phone follows it step by step.

You already use it: WhatsApp, Instagram, Opay, Bolt, Netflix.

Types of software careers

FrontendWhat you see and tap. The shop window.
BackendThe hidden engine that stores data. The kitchen.
MobileApps for Android and iPhone.
AI and dataTeaching computers to learn from data.
Other pathsCybersecurity, games, UI/UX design, DevOps.

Why choose it?

  • High demand: banks, schools, hospitals and shops all need software.
  • Good pay: it grows with your skill, not only your years.
  • Work from anywhere: live in Lagos, work for a company abroad.
  • Solve real problems: transport, farming, health, education, payments.
  • Low cost to start: a phone or laptop, data and discipline.
Part 2 · AI changes the game

AI is your navigator

The internet has an information problem. The question is no longer “where is the information?” but “what do I need, in what order, and how do I apply it?”

AI helps you answer that. Today it can even help you build.

Use it wisely

AI can hallucinate, carry bias, and make you dependent. Always check its answers, and use it to learn faster, not to skip learning.

Prompt engineering in 3 steps

1Context. Say who you are, what you know, and your situation.

2Goal → Constraints → Output. What you want, your limits, and the format you need.

3Iterate. Don’t accept the first answer. Challenge it and refine.

Weak: “Teach me web development.”
Strong: “I’m a beginner with basic computer skills. My goal is a portfolio website in 90 days. I have 1 hour daily. Give me a weekly plan, flag prerequisites, and test me at each milestone.”

Part 4 · Build

1Tell Claude about you

In Claude. Claude will ask you questions, answer them honestly.

My name is [your name]. I run [business name] in [location]. I offer [what you make or offer]. Most of my customers currently find me through Instagram, WhatsApp and referrals.
Goal: I want to grow [business name] into a recognizable brand with a professional online presence.
Act as my business and digital strategy adviser. Before recommending anything, ask me questions about my customers, products, pricing, location, sales channels, competitors, brand identity, goals and resources.
I have a limited budget, so prioritize actions I can do myself with AI and affordable tools.

2Define the website’s job

Same Claude chat, after answering its questions.

I want to create a website for [business name]. Don’t design it yet. Help me work out what the website needs to accomplish based on my business goals.

3Collect design inspiration

Search Pinterest for “[your type of business] website design”. Screenshot 2 to 4 sites you like, upload them to Claude with this:

These are visual references for the kind of website I like. Analyze them. Do not copy them. Explain the common characteristics: typography, spacing, imagery, navigation, layouts, colour usage, visual hierarchy and overall aesthetic.

4Create the design brief

Using what you already know about [business name], turn this into a complete website design brief for my brand.

Claude gives you a document. Copy all of its text.

5Build it in Lovable

Open Lovable. Type this, then paste your whole brief under it.

Help me create a website for my brand using the design brief below:

Send it and wait. Lovable builds a preview you can scroll on your phone.

6Make it yours and publish

Lovable fills in dummy contact details. Replace them, one message at a time:

For the WhatsApp contact, please use this number: [your number with country code]
For Instagram, use this account: [your handle]

Then tap Publish to get your link. Put it in your Instagram bio.

Don’t stop there. Keep improving: swap in real photos, prices and testimonials.

Part 5 · Your next step

This week

1Choose a path. Open roadmap.sh (free) and pick one.

2Learn daily. 30 minutes on one small topic.

3Build one tiny project. Even a simple page about yourself.

4Tell a friend. Learn together.

Start with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, then Python, Git and GitHub. Mix self-learning, bootcamps, university and apprenticeships. A certificate opens the door, projects keep you in the room.

30-day challenge

Pick one problem around you. Research it with AI and talk to real people who have it. Build the smallest solution. Put it in front of people and improve it from their feedback.

Don’t just consume the future. Build it.
